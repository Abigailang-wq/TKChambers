Civil Litigation & Dispute Resolution

We represent clients in a wide range of civil disputes across all levels of the Malaysian courts, guided by the Rules of Court 2012. Our dispute resolution practice is focused on delivering practical, results-driven outcomes, whether through litigation, negotiation, or alternative dispute resolution.

We are experienced in arbitration proceedings under the Arbitration Act 2005, including matters administered by the Asian International Arbitration Centre. Our work spans contractual and commercial disputes, shareholder conflicts, and construction claims, including adjudication under the Construction Industry Payment and Adjudication Act 2012.

In addition, we handle employment disputes, professional negligence claims, and family law matters, including divorce proceedings governed by the Law Reform (Marriage and Divorce) Act 1976. Our practice also extends to intellectual property enforcement with the Intellectual Property Corporation of Malaysia, insolvency proceedings under the Insolvency Act 1967, and complex asset recovery actions.

In strata developments, we advise joint management bodies and management corporations on governance, disputes, and compliance under the Strata Management Act 2013.

Conveyancing & Real Estate

Our firm provides comprehensive legal support in property transactions and real estate development, with all work grounded in the National Land Code 1965. We advise individuals, property developers, and financial institutions on sale and purchase agreements, property transfers, leasing arrangements, and financing structures.

Our firm also maintains an active banking and conveyancing practice, regularly acting for financial institutions in the preparation and execution of loan and security documentation. We also assist with project financing and banking-related documentation, ensuring compliance with regulatory frameworks overseen by Bank Negara Malaysia. We have extensive experience handling loan documentations for banks such as Public Bank Berhad, Public Islamic Bank Berhad, RHB Bank Berhad, RHB Islamic Bank Berhad, and CIMB Bank Berhad, ensuring compliance with banking requirements and the National Land Code 1965. In addition, we routinely act in the discharge of charges for a wide range of financial institutions, including Public Bank Berhad, RHB Bank Berhad, CIMB Bank Berhad, AmBank (M) Berhad, Standard Chartered Bank Malaysia Berhad, AIA Berhad, Affin Bank Berhad, and Bank Simpanan Nasional. Our familiarity with institutional requirements and land registration processes enables us to deliver efficient, accurate, and timely completion of financing and discharge matters.

Post-auction foreclosure completion

The firm also handles post-auction foreclosure matters with a focus on ensuring smooth completion following a successful sale. This includes acting for purchasers in liaising with the auctioning bank to obtain formal acknowledgement of full settlement of the balance purchase price (BPP), as well as attending to the necessary court processes for the transfer of title. We regularly engage with the High Court to facilitate the execution and perfection of statutory transfer documents, including Form 16F, and with the relevant Land Offices for the processing and registration of Form 16I, both pursuant to the National Land Code 1965. This ensures that ownership is properly vested in the purchaser in accordance with legal requirements. Our familiarity with foreclosure procedures and coordination between financial institutions, the courts, and land authorities enables us to complete post-auction transactions efficiently and with minimal delay.

We also provide ancillary support services, including the stamping of legal documents and the conduct of land searches to verify title particulars, ownership status, and existing encumbrances.

Family, Probate & Trust

We guide clients through sensitive personal matters with clarity and discretion. Our services include the preparation of wills in accordance with the Wills Act 1959, ensuring that our clients’ intentions are clearly documented and legally enforceable.

We assist in obtaining grants of probate and letters of administration, enabling the proper administration of estates through the Malaysian courts. In addition, we handle small estate applications before the relevant land office or estate distribution unit, providing a streamlined solution for estates that fall within the statutory threshold. As an appointed agent of Amanah Raya Berhad, we are also able to facilitate estate administration matters through Amanah Raya where appropriate. Beyond this, we offer comprehensive estate planning solutions, including trust structures and succession planning, designed to preserve wealth and minimise future disputes.

Our firm has extensive experience in contested probate matters, particularly in challenging the validity and administration of wills under Malaysian law. We have acted in cases to declare wills invalid on grounds that the testator lacked testamentary capacity, applying the legal principles governing capacity and intention under the Wills Act 1959 and related probate jurisprudence.

Corporate & Advisory

We support businesses at every stage of their growth with practical, commercially focused legal advice. Our corporate practice is grounded in the Companies Act 2016 and supported by our close familiarity with regulatory requirements imposed by the Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia.

From incorporation and governance to restructuring and cross-border transactions, we ensure that our clients operate with clarity and compliance. We regularly advise on contractual matters under the Contracts Act 1950, employment frameworks aligned with the Employment Act 1955 and the Industrial Relations Act 1967, as well as regulatory compliance, including obligations under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

In addition to our core advisory and transactional services, the firm provides a comprehensive range of corporate and commercial support functions. We regularly assist clients in drafting and reviewing internal documents, including letters, memoranda, policies, and business correspondence addressed to employees, suppliers, customers, and other stakeholders. Our work also extends to the preparation and refinement of a wide spectrum of commercial agreements, from standard documents such as non-disclosure agreements, memoranda of understanding, service and consultancy agreements, and tenancy agreements, to more complex arrangements including shareholders’ agreements, investment agreements, joint ventures, franchise and licensing structures, and other strategic commercial contracts.

Beyond documentation, we conduct background searches and corporate intelligence checks, including company profile searches, CTOS checks, insolvency searches, court record searches, and related due diligence enquiries. We also undertake legal due diligence reviews covering corporate, financial, and compliance matters to support transactions and investment decisions. In contentious or pre-contentious scenarios, we assist in issuing letters of demand, particularly in debt recovery matters.

Our approach integrates risk assessment, liability management, and forward-looking compliance strategies, including advisory on consumer-facing obligations under the Consumer Protection Act 1999 and financial regulations overseen by Bank Negara Malaysia.

Foreclosure

Our lawyers are experienced in handling foreclosure proceedings from the initial stages through to auction, providing end-to-end legal support to financial institutions and chargees. This includes the issuance of demand and recall notices, preparation and registration of statutory forms such as Form 16D, and the commencement of foreclosure proceedings by way of Originating Summons in the High Court for titles under a Pendaftar Hakmilik. For titles under a Pentadbir Tanah, we attend to the necessary processes including the preparation and filing of Form 16G, all in accordance with the National Land Code 1965.

We also coordinate closely with appointed valuers to obtain the requisite valuation reports and liaise with auctioneers to facilitate the conduct of public auctions. Our familiarity with procedural requirements, documentation, and stakeholder coordination ensures that foreclosure matters are handled efficiently, with a focus on timely realisation and compliance with statutory requirements.

Criminal Litigation

We provide strategic and robust defence across the full spectrum of criminal matters in Malaysia, including offences under the Penal Code. Our team advises clients from the earliest stages of investigation through to trial and appeal, ensuring that rights are protected at every step under the Criminal Procedure Code.

Our experience extends to complex commercial and white-collar crime, including fraud, criminal breach of trust, and money laundering investigations, often involving agencies such as the Royal Malaysia Police Commercial Crime Investigation Department. We also act in regulatory offence matters arising from non-compliance with laws enforced by authorities such as Securities Commission Malaysia and Bank Negara Malaysia, where the consequences can be both financial and reputational.

The firm also has experience handling regulatory and statutory offences across a range of sectors, including environmental (alam sekitar) prosecutions, fisheries-related cases involving fishing vessels, agricultural offences, and cases involving breaches of planning permission (kebenaran merancang). Our approach focuses on navigating the relevant regulatory frameworks, engaging with enforcement authorities, and advancing a structured defence strategy tailored to the specific statutory regime involved.
